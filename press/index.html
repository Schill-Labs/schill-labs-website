Schill Labs (Schill Labs LLC) is an independent one-person studio in North Carolina, USA, building software, phone apps, and PC games. Its current slate includes FragLedger, a live CS2 match ledger; Live QA, a company QA bot in development; six apps — Mello, Swing Loop, Bloom Doc, MealMacroCoach, Pizza Sidekick and Money Watch; and three PC games coming to the Epic Games Store: Dumpling Shift, a first-person cooking sim; Cruise Magnate, a cruise line tycoon; and Blame Kevin, a stealth comedy.

Product one-liners

  • FragLedger — a live CS2 match ledger built from official Valve demos. Live at fragledger.com.
  • Live QA — a question-and-answer bot for companies, trained on the company's own material.
  • Mello — a calm mobile game for the end of the day: short sessions, nothing to lose.
  • Swing Loop — golf practice built on repetition and tempo, in short focused sessions.
  • MealMacroCoach — food-first macro tracking with official US chain menus, USDA foods and barcode lookup.
  • Pizza Sidekick — an offline pizza-making companion for iPhone: styles, dough calculators, oven profiles and a bake planner.
  • Money Watch — finds subscriptions, price increases, trial endings and bills coming due, with your data kept on your own computer.
  • Bloom Doc — plant identification, diagnosis, and care schedules in one app.
  • Dumpling Shift — a first-person cooking sim: run a dumpling stall in a Chinatown night market. Coming to the Epic Games Store for Windows PC.
  • Blame Kevin — a top-down stealth comedy: prank the office after hours and pin it on Kevin. Coming to the Epic Games Store for Windows on December 11.
  • Cruise Magnate — a cruise line tycoon: start in 1985 with one leased liner and grow the biggest cruise corporation in the world. Coming to the Epic Games Store for Windows.

Naming

It's "Schill Labs" — two words, capital S, capital L. Please don't shorten it to "Schill" or expand it to "Schill Labs Studio".
