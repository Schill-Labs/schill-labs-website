Quick answers

Before you write in.

When do the apps come out?

The apps are all in active development. FragLedger is already live, and Dumpling Shift and Cruise Magnate are coming to the Epic Games Store. The newsletter gets one email the day each next thing ships.

A game crashed or won't start. What should I send?

Email hello@schilllabs.com with the game's name, your Windows or macOS version, and what you were doing. Attach the game's log file if you can: on Windows it is Player.log in the game's folder under %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Schill Labs. The log stays on your computer unless you send it.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email hello@schilllabs.com with the product name in the subject. Screenshots and steps to reproduce help a lot.

How is my data handled?

As little collection as possible, kept on your device where possible, never sold. Details are in the privacy policy.

I found a security issue — what do I do?

Email hello@schilllabs.com with "Security" in the subject. Details are also at /.well-known/security.txt.
