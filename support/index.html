Quick answers

Before you write in.

When do the apps come out?

All four phone apps are in active development. FragLedger is already live. The newsletter gets one email the day each next thing ships.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email hello@schilllabs.com with the product name in the subject. Screenshots and steps to reproduce help a lot.

How is my data handled?

As little collection as possible, kept on your device where possible, never sold. Details are in the privacy policy.

I found a security issue — what do I do?

Email hello@schilllabs.com with "Security" in the subject. Details are also at /.well-known/security.txt.
