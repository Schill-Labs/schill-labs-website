CS2 · Match ledger

Your CS2 matches, kept as a ledger.

FragLedger downloads the official Valve demo from a share code and saves the whole match: scoreboard, rounds, deaths, and a Frag Rating with a published formula. Sign in with Steam once and tracked games stay in one place.

What it does

What's in it.

Official Valve demos

The scoreboard is a summary. The demo is the ordered record. FragLedger is built around the second one.

Share codes

Paste a match share code with your Game Authentication Code. FragLedger resolves it, downloads the demo, and files the match.

Published Frag Rating

A rating with a formula you can read. Components stay open. Nothing hides behind a mystery number.

Missing stays missing

If the parser can't verify a stat, it shows as missing. Zero means a verified zero, not a guess.

Match receipts

Rounds, economy, utility, and duel context stay tied to the same match instead of dissolving into a weekly average.

Pro drills from your deaths

Pro turns those demos into practice cards: the angle, the loadout, and the spot that keeps costing you rounds.

Questions

Common questions.

Is FragLedger live?

Yes. The product is up at fragledger.com. Sign in with Steam to start a ledger.

Do I need Pro?

No. The free tier keeps your match history. Pro is for practice cards, utility from your own demos, and session context.

Is this affiliated with Valve?

No. FragLedger is not affiliated with Valve. Steam and Counter-Strike are trademarks of Valve Corporation.

Where do I get support?

Email hello@schilllabs.com with FragLedger in the subject, or use the help links on the live site.
