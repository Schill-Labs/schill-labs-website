Dumpling
Shift.

Cooking sim First person Single player Windows · macOS

Run a dumpling stall in a Chinatown night market. Fold, steam, fry and boil to order, keep the queue happy, and spend the night's takings on a bigger stall, staff and new dishes. Earn five stars, open your own dumpling house, and win the Golden Steamer.

Epic Games Store page → More about it
The game

One stall, one counter, a whole street to feed.

01 — COOK

Cook with your hands

Hold to fold and let go in the green for a neat dumpling. The steamer is forgiving, the frying pan is fast and burns, the soup pot is slow and never burns.

02 — GROW

From cart to shopfront

Four stall tiers, thirty-one upgrades, staff, bao buns and wonton soup. Name the stall and see it on the sign.

03 — OPEN

Your own dumpling house

Pay off the restaurant and the street gives way to a dining room: four tables, seated parties and a waiter.
