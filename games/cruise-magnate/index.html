Cruise
Magnate.

Cruise line tycoon Management sim Single player Windows

Start in 1985 with almost nothing: a few million dollars and a leased old liner in Miami. Plan the itineraries, grow the fleet, launch new cruise lines and buy out your rivals, until your corporation is the biggest cruise company in the world. Relaxing, but rewarding.

The game

One leased liner, a whole world of ports.

01 — SAIL

A living world map

203 real ports in 18 regions, a 3D globe that blends into the map, and real sea lanes like Suez, Panama and the Inside Passage. Your ships sail them while you watch.

02 — GROW

From one ship to a fleet

Lease your first ship and buy her out later. Choose from 33 ship classes, from 1960s liners to 2040s megaships, and refit them with 36 upgrades.

03 — EXPAND

A corporation of cruise lines

Form a group and launch up to six cruise lines aimed at different markets, each with its own livery. When you are big enough, buy rival lines outright.

04 — SEASONS

Weather and the calendar

Summer and winter itineraries with paid repositioning cruises in between. Hurricanes, typhoons and gales move across the map and disrupt routes.

05 — HEALTH

Trouble at sea

Outbreaks aboard, port health advisories and medical emergencies, and a fleet health program to keep them in check.

06 — LEGACY

Reasons to keep going

The chase up the world rankings, a yearly Captain's Log, 45 story cards, charters, christening ceremonies, a port passport, a headquarters that grows, and 59 awards.
