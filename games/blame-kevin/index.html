The game

Prank the office. Pin it on Kevin.

Brad is a temp. After everyone goes home he stays late, switches the keyboard to Icelandic, autocorrects "regards" to "regrets" and books a 6 a.m. meeting titled "Sync??". Deb from HR walks the floor with a flashlight. When she gets close, it was Kevin.

01 — SNEAK

Stay out of the light

Slip between desks, tiptoe past Deb and throw memos to send her the wrong way. Each company adds a new problem: wall cameras, beeping robot vacuums, Gus the night guard, laser tripwires and motion-sensor lights.

02 — PRANK

Four kinds of prank

From a sticky note to an air horn taped under a chair. Nothing gets broken: the worst that happens is Brad gets escorted out of the building.

03 — BLAME

It was Kevin

Blame Kevin and every Deb on the floor goes to have a word with him. Do it enough and HR lets him go. Kevin keeps getting transferred to wherever Brad goes next.
