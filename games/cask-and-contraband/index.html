Cask &
Contraband.

Brewery sim 1–4 player co-op PC

Start brewing in a garage and grow into the county's biggest beer brand, legally, or by running kegs into the dry county next door. Every batch is brewed by hand, every shortcut has a cost, and it's better with friends.

The game

From a stovetop batch to a beer empire.

01 — BREW

Hands-on craft

Every batch is made by hand: carry the sacks, hit the mash temperature, time the hop additions, clean the tanks. Skill shows in the glass.

02 — GROW

Garage to brewhouse

Grow from a garage stovetop to an automated brewhouse, then hire workers and machines to take over the chores while you run the business.

03 — RISK

Heat is a resource

Skipping licenses and running kegs pays more but builds Heat with the law. Managing it is part of the game, not a fail state. Legit Mode turns the illegal side off.

04 — CO-OP

Better with friends

Up to four players share one company: one brews, one sells, one drives, one keeps the cops busy.

05 — RECIPES

Your own beer

Mix malts, hops, yeast and adjuncts, name the beer, design the label and see it on the shelves.

06 — TOWN

A small town to win

Brew, deliver, and build a name around town, with a rival brewery and a shady bar owner in the dry county who pays double with no questions.
