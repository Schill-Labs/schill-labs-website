Devlog 001

The lights are on.

August 6, 2026 · Schill Labs

Schill Labs has a website now. You're on it. This first post is mostly a marker — the "grand opening" sign in the window — but here's an honest rundown of where everything stands.

The apps

Mello is the furthest along conceptually: a calm game for the end of the day, and the project that taught me what I don't want in mobile games. Swing Loop came out of an itch for a practice tool for something I actually practice, and Bloom Doc exists because I got tired of guessing why leaves turn yellow. They are in active development, targeting iOS and Android.

What's next

Apps roll out one at a time, each when it's actually done. I'll post here when there's something real to show — builds, screenshots, dates — and skip the filler updates in between.
