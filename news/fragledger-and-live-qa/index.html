Devlog 002

Two more names on the board.

August 12, 2026 · Schill Labs

The site used to read like a phone-app studio with a game in the back room. That's incomplete. Two software products belong on the board, so they're on it now.

FragLedger is live

FragLedger is a CS2 match ledger. You paste a share code, it pulls the official Valve demo, and it keeps the match: rounds, deaths, receipts, and a Frag Rating with a published formula. The product lives at fragledger.com. This site is just the studio listing.

Live QA is a company bot

Live QA is a question-and-answer bot for companies. It answers from the company's own docs, help pages, and policies so customers and staff stop hunting through folders. It is still in development. When there's a real trial, the newsletter hears first.

Everything else

The four phone apps are still cooking. Nothing about those changed today except that they share a site with two more names.
