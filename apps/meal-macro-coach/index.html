Food · Macros

Macro tracking that starts with the food.

MealMacroCoach shows what is left of today's calories, protein, carbs and fat, and ranks foods by what still fits. Official US chain menus, USDA foods and barcode lookup are built in, and your food diary stays on your device.

What it does

What's in it.

What's left today

Today shows your remaining calories, protein, carbs and fat at a glance.

Search ranks what fits

Food search puts what still fits your day first, and every entry can be undone.

Official chain menus

Official US chain menus ship inside the app, so a drive-through order isn't a guess.

USDA and barcodes

USDA foods and Open Food Facts barcode lookup for meals that aren't on a menu.

Diet filters

Filter foods and menus to match the way you eat.

Plans for a goal

Premium training plans for a cut or a race.

When does MealMacroCoach come out?

Where is my food diary kept?

On your device. Account sync is optional.
