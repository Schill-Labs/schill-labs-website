Food · Cooking

A pizza-making companion that works offline.

Pizza Sidekick plans the dough and the bake for the equipment you actually have. Nineteen pizza styles, fourteen oven profiles, dough calculators and a timer, all on your iPhone with no connection needed.

What it does

What's in it.

19 pizza styles

Nineteen styles with dough presets, five scalable topping combinations and seven cooking approaches.

Your equipment

Pick your oven, pizza oven or grill and your steel, stone or pan once. The bake planner only uses what you have.

Dough math

Baker's percentages, sourdough and yeasted preferment splits, pan-size scaling and a water-temperature calculator.

14 oven profiles

Oven profiles with notes from owner reviews, and your oven saved for next time.

A searchable handbook

Guides for every step, each with links to its sources.

Timer and units

A bake timer with an optional notification, in Fahrenheit or Celsius.

Questions

Common questions.

When does Pizza Sidekick come out?

It's in development for iPhone (iOS 17 or later). The newsletter gets one email the day it's out.

Does it need a connection?

No. It works offline, and your saved styles and oven choice stay on your phone.

Are the recipes tested?

Dough presets, ingredient suggestions and bake times are starting points, not tested recipes. Fermentation depends on your flour, starter or yeast, time and temperature.

Will it have ads?

It is planned as a free app with a banner ad. Its privacy details will be added to the privacy policy before it launches.
