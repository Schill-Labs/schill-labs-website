Finance · Subscriptions

Every subscription, price hike and bill coming due.

Money Watch reads your bank and card transactions and finds the recurring charges: subscriptions, price increases, trials about to end, and the same service billed on two accounts. Your data stays on your own computer.

What it does

What's in it.

Subscriptions, found

Recurring charges are detected for you. Add any bill that isn't in the feed, and recategorize anything.

Price hikes and trials

Flags price increases, trials and intro prices about to end, and when switching to annual billing would save money.

Billed twice

Spots the same merchant charging you on more than one account.

Coming due

A digest of renewals and card due dates, plus a cash runway that knows when you get paid.

Debt HQ

Avalanche versus snowball on your own numbers, what-if extra payments, and interest saved.

Budgets and goals

Budgets, cash flow, net worth and savings goals. It never moves your money.

Questions

Common questions.

How does it connect to my bank?

You sign in through Plaid, so Money Watch never sees your bank password. You can also import CSV or OFX exports instead.

Where is my data kept?

In a database on your own computer. There is no Money Watch cloud account.

Is it financial advice?

No. It points out patterns in your own transactions. It is not an adviser and does not move money.

What does it run on?

It's in development as a web app that runs on your computer. On a phone, you add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app.
