Chill · Game

A calm game for the end of the day.

Mello is a small game for winding down. Sessions are short, the pace is slow, and there's nothing to lose, so it never punishes you for putting the phone down.

What it does

What's in it.

Short sessions

A session fits in a few minutes and ends cleanly. You're never stuck mid-anything.

Slow pace

Ambient scenes and sound that don't rush you. It moves at whatever speed you do.

No streaks or lives

Nothing resets, nothing expires, nothing guilts you for skipping a week.

One-handed play

Everything works with one thumb, on the couch or on the train.

Dark palette

The whole game lives in dusk colors, so it's easy on the eyes at night.

Nothing nagging you

No pop-ups begging you to come back. Mello is there when you want it.
