Lifestyle · Plants

Keep your plants alive.

Bloom Doc helps you figure out what plant you have, what's wrong with it, and what to do about it. Identification, diagnosis, and care schedules in one app.

What it does

What's in it.

Identify from a photo

Snap a picture and get an ID, plus the basics on what that plant needs.

Diagnose problems

Yellow leaves, brown tips, droop. Describe or photograph it and get likely causes.

Care schedules

Watering and feeding reminders matched to the plant, not a generic timer.

Quiet reminders

Nudges arrive batched and are easy to snooze. No 7am guilt trips.

Your plant list

Every plant you own in one place, with notes and history.

Works at any scale

One pothos or a whole jungle, the app keeps up.
