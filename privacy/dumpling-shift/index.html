Dumpling Shift privacy policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

The short version: Dumpling Shift has no ads, no analytics of our own and no tracking, and we never sell information. Your save stays on your computer. If you sign in with a store account, the game uses it only for sign-in and achievements.

Who we are

Dumpling Shift is developed and published by Schill Labs LLC, an independent studio registered in North Carolina, USA (about the game). This policy covers the Dumpling Shift game on PC and Mac, in its Epic Games Store and Steam versions. For this website, see the Schill Labs website privacy policy. Contact us at hello@schilllabs.com, or by mail at Schill Labs LLC, 4030 Wake Forest Rd Ste 349, Raleigh, NC 27609, USA.

What information the game captures, and how

  • Your game data. Created as you play: your save, settings, local scores and achievement progress. The game writes it to a folder on your computer.
  • A diagnostic log. The game writes a log file on your computer that records errors, your operating system, graphics card and display settings. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.
  • Your Epic Games account (Epic version, only if you sign in). When you allow Dumpling Shift to access your Epic Games account, the game receives your Epic account ID, display name, preferred language and the display names of linked accounts, plus a player ID used only by this game.
  • Your Steam account (Steam version). When the game connects to Steam, it reads your Steam display name.
  • Achievement unlocks and scores. When you unlock an achievement, the game records it with your player ID. In the Steam version, your daily-night score is also recorded.
  • Hardware statistics. The Unity engine that Dumpling Shift is built with may send anonymous technical details to Unity Technologies: your operating system, processor, graphics card, memory and screen size. It does not include your name, account or game progress.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, such as a diagnostic log.

Schill Labs runs no servers for the game. The game collects nothing else: no location, no contacts, no microphone or camera, no advertising IDs.

How we use it

  • Your game data and settings let you carry on where you left off.
  • Your account details are used to sign you in, show your name, and record your achievements (and, on Steam, your daily-night scores).
  • Diagnostic logs and support messages are used to answer you and to fix problems in the game.

We do not use any of it for advertising, profiling, or anything unrelated to running the game and supporting you.

How it is shared

  • The store you play through. Sign-in, your player ID, and your achievement unlocks go to Epic Games (Epic version) or Valve (Steam version), because those services provide sign-in and achievements. Your account with them is covered by their own privacy policies: Epic Games, Steam.
  • Unity Technologies receives the anonymous hardware statistics described above.
  • Our email provider holds support messages sent to hello@schilllabs.com.

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Game data and logs on your computer: until you delete them or uninstall the game. The save and log are in the game's folder in your user account's application data (under AppData\LocalLow on Windows, under your Library folder on a Mac), and settings are kept with your user preferences.
  • Achievement records linked to your player ID: for as long as the game is offered, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Support email and anything attached: up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without signing in to a store account; achievements earned offline are kept in your save.
  • You can delete your game data at any time by removing those files, or by starting a new game, which replaces your save.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete information we hold about you, including achievement records linked to your player ID. Email hello@schilllabs.com. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Dumpling Shift is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
