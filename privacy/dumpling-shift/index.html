Dumpling Shift privacy policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Schill Labs develops Dumpling Shift. This policy explains how the game handles your information. For information about visiting this website, see the Schill Labs website privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your save, settings, local scores, and earned achievements on your device. The game also writes diagnostic logs that can help identify errors. You can play without completing an Epic Games sign-in. Your local game files stay on your device until you remove them, subject to any backup or sync service you use.

Epic Games sign-in and achievements

When you use the Epic Games version, the game can sign in through the Epic Games Launcher or Epic's sign-in page. It requests Epic's Basic Profile permission, which makes your Epic account ID, display name, language, and linked account display names available to the app. The game uses an Epic account ID and an authentication token to sign in to Epic Online Services and obtain a game-specific player ID.

The game uses that player ID to sync achievement unlocks with Epic Online Services. Achievements you earned offline may sync the next time you sign in. Epic stores those achievement records and may display them according to your Epic account settings. Epic explains how it handles account and service data and how to manage your information in the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Steam features

When the Steam version connects to Steam, the game can read your Steam display name and send achievement unlocks and eligible scores to Steam. Steam may show scores on leaderboards. See the Steam Privacy Policy for Valve's handling of Steam account and service data.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we use your email address, message, and any files you send to respond and troubleshoot. A diagnostic log you share can contain details about your device, platform sign-in, and game errors. The Schill Labs website privacy policy describes how long we keep support correspondence.

Contact hello@schilllabs.com to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information Schill Labs holds. For your platform account, privacy settings, or information held by Epic or Steam, use that platform's privacy controls or contact the platform directly.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data practices change.
