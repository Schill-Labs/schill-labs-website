Cruise Magnate privacy policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

The short version: Cruise Magnate has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell information. Your saves and settings stay on your computer, and the game plays fully offline. If you start it from the Epic Games Launcher, it signs in with your Epic account only to unlock achievements.

Who we are

Cruise Magnate is developed and published by Schill Labs LLC, an independent studio registered in North Carolina, USA (about the game). This policy covers the Cruise Magnate game for Windows PC, in its Epic Games Store version and the Steam version that follows it. For this website, see the Schill Labs website privacy policy. Contact us at michael@schilllabs.com, or by mail at Schill Labs LLC, 4030 Wake Forest Rd Ste 349, Raleigh, NC 27609, USA.

What information the game captures, and how

  • Your game data. Created as you play: your autosave and manual save (your company name, fleet, finances and progress) and your settings, such as volume, display options, interface size and key bindings. The game writes saves to a folder on your computer and keeps settings with your Windows user preferences.
  • A diagnostic log. The game writes a log file on your computer that records errors and basic system details such as your operating system and graphics card. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.
  • Your Epic Games account (Epic version, when started from the Epic Games Launcher). The game signs in to Epic Account Services with the sign-in the launcher hands it, asking only for Epic's basic profile permission. Through it, the game receives your Epic account ID, display name, preferred language and the display names of linked accounts, plus a player ID used only by this game, which Epic creates the first time you play.
  • Achievement unlocks. When you earn an award, the game records the unlock with your player ID (Epic version) or on your Steam account (Steam version).
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, such as a diagnostic log.

Schill Labs runs no servers for the game. The game collects nothing else: no location, no contacts, no microphone or camera, no advertising IDs. The Unity engine's analytics, crash and performance reporting and hardware statistics are all turned off.

How we use it

  • Your saves and settings let you carry on where you left off.
  • Your Epic account and player ID are used only to sign you in and record your achievements. The game does not show or store your name.
  • Diagnostic logs and support messages are used to answer you and to fix problems in the game.

We do not use any of it for advertising, profiling, or anything unrelated to running the game and supporting you.

How it is shared

  • The store you play through. Sign-in, your player ID and your achievement unlocks go to Epic Games (Epic version) or Valve (Steam version), because those services provide sign-in and achievements. Your account with them is covered by their own privacy policies: Epic Games, Steam.
  • Photo credits. Port photos in the game come from Wikimedia Commons. If you click a photo's credit or source link, your web browser opens that page on Wikimedia Commons, which is covered by Wikimedia's own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you click.
  • Our email provider holds support messages sent to michael@schilllabs.com.

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Saves, settings and logs on your computer: until you delete them or uninstall the game. Saves and the log are in AppData\LocalLow\Schill Labs\Cruise Magnate in your Windows user folder, and settings are stored in your Windows user registry under Software\Schill Labs\Cruise Magnate.
  • Achievement records linked to your player ID: for as long as the game is offered, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Support email and anything attached: up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without signing in. If the game is not started from the Epic Games Launcher, or the sign-in fails, it never connects to Epic, and the awards you earn are kept in your save. The next time you start it from the launcher, it unlocks every award you already hold.
  • You can delete your game data at any time by removing those files, or by starting a new game, which replaces your save.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete information we hold about you, including achievement records linked to your player ID. Email michael@schilllabs.com. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Cruise Magnate is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
