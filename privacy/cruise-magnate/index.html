Cruise Magnate privacy policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

The short version: Cruise Magnate has no ads, no analytics of our own and no tracking, and we never sell information. Your saves and settings stay on your computer. The game has no account or sign-in of its own, and Schill Labs runs no servers for it.

Who we are

Cruise Magnate is developed and published by Schill Labs LLC, an independent studio registered in North Carolina, USA (about the game). This policy covers the Cruise Magnate game for Windows PC, including the versions sold through PC game stores. For this website, see the Schill Labs website privacy policy. Contact us at hello@schilllabs.com, or by mail at Schill Labs LLC, 4030 Wake Forest Rd Ste 349, Raleigh, NC 27609, USA.

What information the game captures, and how

  • Your game data. Created as you play: your autosave and manual save (company name, fleet, finances and progress) and your settings, such as volume, display options and view preferences. The game writes saves to a folder on your computer and keeps settings with your user preferences.
  • A diagnostic log. The Unity engine writes a log file on your computer that records errors and basic system details such as your operating system and graphics card. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.
  • Achievements (Steam version). When the Steam client is running, the game unlocks achievements on your Steam account as you earn awards. It does not read your Steam profile, display name or friends list.
  • Hardware statistics. The Unity engine that Cruise Magnate is built with may send anonymous technical details to Unity Technologies: your operating system, processor, graphics card, memory and screen size. It does not include your name, account or game progress.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, such as a diagnostic log.

The game collects nothing else: no location, no contacts, no microphone or camera, no advertising IDs. It plays fully offline.

How we use it

  • Your saves and settings let you carry on where you left off.
  • Achievement unlocks are recorded so they show on your store account.
  • Diagnostic logs and support messages are used to answer you and to fix problems in the game.

We do not use any of it for advertising, profiling, or anything unrelated to running the game and supporting you.

How it is shared

  • The store you play through. In the Steam version, achievement unlocks go to Valve, because Steam provides achievements. Your store account, purchases and the store's launcher are covered by that store's own privacy policy, for example Steam or Epic Games.
  • Unity Technologies receives the anonymous hardware statistics described above.
  • Photo credits. Port photos in the game come from Wikimedia Commons. If you click a photo's credit or source link, your web browser opens that page on Wikimedia Commons, which is covered by Wikimedia's own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you click.
  • Our email provider holds support messages sent to hello@schilllabs.com.

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Saves, settings and logs on your computer: until you delete them or uninstall the game. Saves and the log are in AppData\LocalLow\Schill Labs\Cruise Magnate in your Windows user folder, and settings are stored in your Windows user registry under Software\Schill Labs\Cruise Magnate.
  • Achievements: on your store account, under that store's own policy.
  • Support email and anything attached: up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without the Steam client running; awards you earn are kept in your save and sync to Steam the next time you load the game with Steam running.
  • You can delete your game data at any time by removing those files, or by starting a new game, which replaces your save.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete information we hold about you. Email hello@schilllabs.com. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Cruise Magnate is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, for example if a store version adds sign-in or online features, we will update this page and its effective date before that version ships.
