Blame Kevin privacy policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

The short version: Blame Kevin has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell information. Your save stays on your computer. The Epic and Steam versions use your store account only to record achievements.

Who we are

Blame Kevin is developed and published by Schill Labs LLC, an independent studio registered in North Carolina, USA (about the game). This policy covers the Blame Kevin game for Windows, in its Epic Games Store and Steam versions, and any browser version we offer. For this website, see the Schill Labs website privacy policy. Contact us at hello@schilllabs.com, or by mail at Schill Labs LLC, 4030 Wake Forest Rd Ste 349, Raleigh, NC 27609, USA.

What information the game captures, and how

  • Your game data. Created as you play: your progress, stamps, supplies, settings and achievement progress. On Windows the game writes it to a save file on your computer, in %APPDATA%\Blame Kevin\save\, with a backup copy next to it. In a browser it is kept in that browser's local storage.
  • A diagnostic log. The game writes a log file, %APPDATA%\Blame Kevin\log.txt, that records errors and whether the store sign-in and achievements worked. In the Steam version it also records your Steam display name. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.
  • Your Epic Games account (Epic version). When you start the game from the Epic Games Launcher, it signs in with the Epic account the launcher is using. Epic gives the game basic profile access (your Epic account ID, display name and preferred language) and a player ID used only by this game. The game uses these only to record your achievements.
  • Your Steam account (Steam version). When the game connects to Steam, it reads your Steam display name and records achievements with your Steam account.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, such as the log file.

Schill Labs runs no servers for the game. The game collects nothing else: no location, no contacts, no microphone or camera, no advertising IDs, and there are no in-game purchases.

How we use it

  • Your game data and settings let you carry on where you left off.
  • Your store account is used to sign the game in and record your achievements.
  • Logs and support messages are used to answer you and to fix problems in the game.

We do not use any of it for advertising, profiling, or anything unrelated to running the game and supporting you.

How it is shared

  • The store you play through. Sign-in, your player ID and your achievement unlocks go to Epic Games (Epic version) or Valve (Steam version), because those services provide sign-in and achievements. On Steam, the Steam client may also sync your save folder with Steam Cloud. Your account with them is covered by their own privacy policies: Epic Games, Steam.
  • Our email provider holds support messages sent to hello@schilllabs.com.

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Game data and logs on your computer: until you delete them. Uninstalling the game may leave the %APPDATA%\Blame Kevin folder in place; delete it to remove everything.
  • Achievement records: kept by Epic Games or Valve with your store account, under their policies.
  • Support email and anything attached: up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your game data at any time by removing those files. The game's Export button gives you a save code you can keep as a backup.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete information we hold about you. Email hello@schilllabs.com. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Blame Kevin is rated for everyone and is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
