The studio

About Schill Labs.

Schill Labs is one person — me. I build software, phone apps, and a PC game. This page is the honest version of what that means.

What this is

Schill Labs is a one-person software studio. There's no office and no team photo to show you. There's a backlog, a lot of coffee, and a rule that nothing ships until I'd be happy paying for it myself.

The software side has two products. FragLedger is live: a CS2 match ledger built from official Valve demos. Live QA is a question-and-answer bot for companies, still in development.

The app side currently has four projects: Mello, a calm game for the end of the day; Dryfire Trainer, structured dry-fire practice; Swing Loop, golf practice built on repetition; and Bloom Doc, for keeping plants alive. Each one exists because I wanted it and couldn't find a version I liked.

The game side has three projects. Dumpling Shift is a first-person cooking sim about running a dumpling stall in a Chinatown night market, coming to the Epic Games Store for PC and Mac. Schill Labs develops and publishes it. Cruise Magnate is a cruise line tycoon for Windows PC: start in 1985 with one leased liner and grow the biggest cruise corporation in the world. The third is a western settlement builder for Steam, the long-haul project, which doesn't have a public name yet.

FragLedger is the first thing that's actually out. The four phone apps, Live QA, and all three games are still in development. When the next one launches, the newsletter is where it gets announced.

How I work

Things ship when they're done, not when a quarter ends. Everything I release stays supported — the support email goes to me, and bugs get fixed by the person who wrote them. And I don't do dark patterns: no fake urgency, no streak guilt, no trackers on this site.

The name

"Schill" is from my last name. "Labs" is because half of what I build starts as an experiment, and only the ones that survive testing get released.
