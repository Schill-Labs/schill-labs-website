Support

Last updated October 27, 2026 · Schill Labs LLC

MealMacroCoach is a food logging, meal planning, grocery, and fitness app from Schill Labs LLC. It is not a medical device.

Help in the app

Open You for account, privacy, and subscription controls. You can use the local diary without an account. Optional sign-in syncs recipes, planner data, and manually entered diary data. Imported Health records stay on the device.

To delete an active account and its cloud diary, open You → Account → Delete account and diary and confirm. That does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Manage or cancel store subscriptions in the store account settings.
