Privacy Policy

Last updated August 25, 2026 · Schill Labs

Diet App is a local-first food, training, and leftover-macros app from Schill Labs.

What we store

The diary (food logs, workouts, body metrics, water, sleep, custom foods, prices you type, and profile stats) lives on this device or, on the website, in this browser. We do not create an account for you. We do not operate a nutrition API. Chain menus ship inside the app from official brand PDFs and pages.

What we do not collect

We do not collect name, email, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not sell data. We do not run third-party analytics or ad SDKs in this version. We do not upload Apple Health or Health Connect data.

Deletion and retention

Deleting the app or clearing this website’s site data deletes the on-device diary. We cannot restore it. We do not keep a server copy.

Apple Health and Health Connect

On iPhone, with your permission, the app reads workouts, weight, body fat, waist, sleep, steps, resting heart rate, and water from Apple Health on this device. Garmin, Fitbit, WHOOP, Oura, Apple Watch, Strava, Peloton, Withings, Renpho, and similar apps can share into Apple Health; we do not log into those services. On Android, the same types are read from Health Connect. This data stays in the on-device diary. The website cannot read HealthKit. You can turn access off in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Purchases

Yearly premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple’s App Store on iOS (product ID diet_app_premium_yearly). Apple processes that payment. We do not receive your card number. Leftover ranking and official menus stay free. The website does not sell subscriptions; subscribe in the iOS app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Contact: schilllabs.com/diet
